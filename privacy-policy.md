# Privacy Policy for Longhaul Binge

**Last Updated:** September 2026

## Overview

Longhaul Binge is an offline video player for personal Plex Media Servers. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect, sell, or share personal data.** Longhaul Binge:

- Does not send telemetry, analytics, or crash reports
- Does not require an account or sign-in
- Does not track usage, downloads, or viewing habits
- Does not serve advertisements
- Does not use third-party analytics libraries

## How Your Data Is Handled

### Server Credentials
- Your Plex server URL and authentication token are stored **only in your device's Keychain**
- They are never transmitted anywhere except to your own Plex server
- They are never stored in iCloud, backed up, or synchronized

### Downloaded Content
- Episodes you download are stored **only on your device**
- Downloaded metadata (title, poster, watched status) is stored locally in the app's container
- Artwork is cached on your device for offline viewing
- All data remains under your control

### Network Activity
- The app only communicates with your personal Plex Media Server
- No communication with our servers (we have none)
- No communication with any third party

## Permissions

Longhaul Binge requests these device permissions:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| **Local Network** | To discover and connect to your Plex server on your home network |
| **Off-Device Storage** | To cache downloaded episodes and artwork for offline access |

Your consent to these permissions is requested by iOS when you first use the app.

## Third-Party Services

Longhaul Binge does not use:
- Analytics libraries
- Crash reporting tools
- Advertising networks
- Cloud storage services
- Third-party servers of any kind

The only external service the app connects to is **your own Plex Media Server**, which you control.

## Your Rights

You have full control over all data stored by Longhaul Binge:

- **Export:** All data is stored in standard formats (local files and Keychain entries)
- **Delete:** Uninstalling the app deletes all data from your device
- **Review:** Open the app's documents folder on your Mac to inspect downloaded files
